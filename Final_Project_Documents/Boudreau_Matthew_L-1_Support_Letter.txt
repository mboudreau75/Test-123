ABC Inc. respectfully requests authorization to employ Alan Delevie in the qualifying L‑1A manager position of Manager, Reefer Container Operations in Elizabeth, New Jersey.  He will report directly to Gary Gabbs, Director, Reefer Container Operations – NAM.  His annual salary will be $100,000.

I.  The Petitioner and the Qualifying Corporate Relationship

-  Corporate Relationship

ABC Inc. is a wholly‑owned subsidiary of the ultimate parent company, CAB Inc.  BCA Inc. is also a wholly‑owned subsidiary of CAB Inc.  Therefore the companies are affiliates.

-  The Petitioner

CAB Inc. was founded in 1989 as a maritime shipping and logistics entity focused on European and North American trade.  Its core business is the shipping of goods requiring temperature‑controlled containers, known as “reefer” containers.  ABC Inc. operates the Port of New York and New Jersey’s largest refrigerated container capacity and manages the full spectrum of reefer‑container operations, from terminal infrastructure to cargo handling and compliance.

II.  The Qualifying Supervisor Position Abroad: Supervisor, Reefer Container Operations

The Supervisor, Reefer Container Operations role is a managerial position.  In partnership with Headquarters and shipping lines, the supervisor designs and implements commercial strategy for reefer cargo, manages pricing, and optimizes trade revenue and volumes.  The role also monitors and analyzes agent performances, drives commercial relationships with key accounts, and reviews service performance.

**Managerial capacity:** The supervisor has overall responsibility and accountability for the reefer cargo segment, establishes pricing and service policies, and makes strategic decisions that directly influence the company’s profitability.  He/she directly supervises a cross‑functional team that includes four Reefer Pricers, six Cargoflow Experts, one Key Account Manager, and three technical experts.  These duties satisfy the USCIS definition of managerial capacity under 8 C.F.R. § 214.2(l)(2)(i) and the managerial requirements outlined in the USCIS Policy Manual, Vol. 2, Part L, Chapter 3.

**Direct supervision:** The supervisory hierarchy is: Supervisor > (4 Pricers + 6 Experts + 1 Key Account Manager + 3 Technical Experts).  The supervisor is authorized to hire, terminate, promote, and discipline employees.

**Experience abroad:** Alan Delevie has worked continuously for BCA Inc. in this manager‑level role for an uninterrupted period of more than two years (January 1, 2024 – November 1, 2026), meeting the L‑1A requirement of at least one continuous year of employment in a managerial, executive, or specialized knowledge capacity within the preceding three years.

III.  Proposed L‑1A Manager Position in the United States: Manager, Reefer Container Operations

In the United States, the Manager, Reefer Container Operations role is a senior managerial position responsible for leading all reefer operations and maintenance activities across the terminal.  The manager ensures safe, reliable, and efficient temperature‑controlled cargo handling, oversees power infrastructure and monitoring systems, coordinates with Operations, Planning, Vessel Operations, Yard, Rail, and Customer Service teams, and drives preventive, predictive, and corrective maintenance programs.

**Managerial capacity:** The manager directly supervises the Reefer Operations team, which includes two Specialists, Reefer Container Operations.  These specialists, in turn, supervise eight Technicians, Reefer Container Operations.  The manager is responsible for hiring, training, performance management, and the overall strategic direction of the reefer operations function—meeting L‑1A managerial criteria of direct supervision and overall responsibility for a department or function.

**Specialized knowledge (beneficial):** While L‑1A does not require specialized knowledge, Alan Delevie’s deep understanding of ABC Inc.’s proprietary reefer monitoring platforms, Power BI reporting tools, and terminal transformation initiatives provides advanced operational excellence capabilities that further support the visa request.

Conclusion

ABC Inc. respectfully requests authorization to employ Alan Delevie in this qualifying L‑1A manager position for a period of three (3) years.  Based on the evidence presented, Alan Delevie meets all USCIS criteria for L‑1A classification, including continuous managerial employment abroad for more than one year and direct supervisory responsibilities in the United States.
