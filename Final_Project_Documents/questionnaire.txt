U.S. Position Job Description:

Lead critical refrigerated cargo operations that directly impact customer satisfaction, cargo integrity, regulatory compliance, and terminal performance. Work across operational, maintenance, engineering, planning, and customer-facing teams to drive reliability and continuous improvement. Utilize advanced operational systems, reefer monitoring platforms, and Power BI reporting to support data-driven decision making.
Contribute to terminal transformation and operational excellence initiatives. ABC Inc. operates the Port of New York and New Jersey's largest refrigerated container capacity. As Manager, Reefer Container Operations, you will be responsible for the overall management of reefer operations, maintenance activities, and refrigerated container infrastructure across the terminal.
You will lead safe, reliable, and efficient temperature-controlled cargo handling while ensuring compliance with customer requirements, regulatory standards, and operational expectations.
Key Responsibilities
Manage all reefer operations and maintenance activities across the terminal, ensuring the safe, reliable, and efficient handling of temperature-controlled cargo. Ensure continuous availability and reliability of reefer power infrastructure, monitoring systems, racks, outlets, and related assets while coordinating reefer capacity and operational readiness. Oversee pre-trip inspections (PTI), reefer monitoring, temperature control, fault diagnostics, repairs, alarm response activities, and emergency interventions to protect cargo integrity.
Coordinate closely with Operations, Planning, Vessel Operations, Yard, Rail, and Customer Service teams to ensure uninterrupted reefer services and compliance with customer and shipping line requirements. Develop and execute preventive, predictive, and corrective maintenance programs; monitor equipment performance, conduct root cause analysis, and implement reliability improvement initiatives.
Ensure compliance with USDA, CBP, FDA, customer requirements, cold-treatment protocols, electrical safety regulations, environmental standards, ISO requirements, and terminal operating procedures. Promote a strong safety culture through safety observations, toolbox talks, audits, risk assessments, incident investigations, and corrective and preventive actions.
Manage reefer operational and maintenance budgets, develop CAPEX and OPEX forecasts, support procurement activities, and oversee contractor, vendor, and service agreement performance. Lead, coach, and develop reefer technicians, foremen, supervisors, and support staff while fostering accountability, engagement, workforce development, and continuous improvement.
Drive Lean, 5S, operational excellence, digitalization, and terminal transformation initiatives while developing and tracking KPIs to improve service levels, asset reliability, safety performance, and operational efficiency.

Directly supervise the Reefer Operations team, including two (2) Specialists, Reefer Container Operations, who in turn oversee eight (8) Technicians, Reefer Container Operations.
